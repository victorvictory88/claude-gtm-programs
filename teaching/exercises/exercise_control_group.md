# Exercise: Design a Control Group Rollout

**Time:** 15 minutes  
**Work alone or in pairs**

## Scenario (fictional)

Acme Energy is evaluating a new technical discovery playbook for AMER mid-market energy accounts. Leadership wants every rep to use it tomorrow. You will design a staged rollout with a control group so uplift is legible.

## Task

Fill this template. Do not invent win rates or pipeline dollars. Use TBD with a named owner when data is missing.

```
Experiment name:
Segment / geo:
Window (start / end):

Baseline primary metric:
  value or TBD + owner:
  source system:

Hypothesis (one sentence):

Treatment:
  who:
  what they run:

Control:
  who:
  what stays legacy:

Assignment method (random / stratified / other):
Sample sizes (or TBD):

Staged rollout:
1. Stage / gate:
2. Stage / gate:
3. Stage / gate:

Stop / scale rules:
  pause if:
  iterate if:
  scale if:

Causal clarity:
  what the control filters out:
  what still confounds:

Owners:
  Program:
  Sales sponsor:
  Enablement:
  Analytics:
```

## Done when

- Treatment and control are both named
- At least one staged gate is measurable or explicitly TBD with an owner
- You can explain in one sentence why blasting to 100% of reps would make uplift illegible

## Debrief (facilitator)

Compare designs. Prefer the one with clearer causal isolation over the one that maximizes short-term coverage.
